# Internal Workflow: Discovery Closeout

1. Evaluate each success criterion against evidence.
2. Generate a durable `DISC-xxxx.md` record in Governance and surface it for review.
3. Interview separately for knowledge promotion and code promotion.
4. Detect related experiments and recommend synthesis when useful.
5. Only after the record is approved, present Archive, Record + Delete, or Keep Active with a recommendation.
6. Do not delete or archive remote repos. Do not commit.
