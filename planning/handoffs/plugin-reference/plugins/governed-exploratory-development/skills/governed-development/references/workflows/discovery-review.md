# Internal Workflow: Discovery Review

1. Evaluate each success criterion against Conformance Proofs, including negative results and uncertainty.
2. Generate a durable Discovery Report at `discoveries/DISC-xxxx.md` in the Governance repository and surface it for review.
3. Record independent Discovery Disposition choices for Discovery Governance Promotion and Discovery Code Promotion; both may be selected, declined, or deferred.
4. Detect related Discovery Implementations and recommend a Discovery Comparison when useful.
5. After surfacing the Discovery Report, present the independent repository retention choice: Archive, Report + Delete, or Keep Active, with a recommendation. An additional report-approval gate is not required. Record the developer’s decisions in the report; Keep Active does not set closed status.
6. Do not delete or archive remote repositories. Do not commit.
