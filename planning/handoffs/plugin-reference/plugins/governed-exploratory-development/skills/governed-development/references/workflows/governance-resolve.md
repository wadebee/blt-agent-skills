# Internal Workflow: Governance Proposal Resolution

1. Present the pending proposal and relevant authority context.
2. Require a human choice: accept, modify, or reject.
3. Move the proposal to `proposals/resolved/` and add minimal resolution metadata.
4. For accepted/modified proposals, prepare separate normative edits and conduct Product impact assessment.
5. Preserve rejected reasoning.
6. Never modify Product or its submodule automatically. Do not commit.
